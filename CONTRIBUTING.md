# Contributing

* Open an issue to claim a question from `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, report a discrepancy, or point to prior art.
* Pull requests welcome: code, data, proofs, Lean. Keep claims honest: separate what is proved, what is checked exhaustively, and what is a pattern in data.
* **Credit.** Everyone who contributes something that is used is named in `CREDITS.md` (tell me how you want to be listed). If any of this ever becomes a prize submission, Wolfram's rules require a verified human submitter and naming all contributors; we will agree how credit and any prize are handled **before** anything is submitted, and in writing.
* Disagreement is welcome. A clear counterexample or a bug report is a contribution.
* By contributing you agree your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`).
